Kingdom Flow Movement Support

Help with the KFM workout tracker

Need help or found a bug?
Email kingdomflowmovement@gmail.com. Tell us what you were doing, what you expected, and what happened. A screenshot helps. Include your iPhone model and iOS version if you can.

Common questions

Where is my data stored? Can I use the app offline?

Everything is stored on your phone, and the app works fully offline. Nothing is sent to a server.

I got a new phone. Can I move my workouts?

Your data isn’t stored online, so it doesn’t move automatically. Use the backup option in Settings (when available) to save your data to a file and restore it on the new phone.

I deleted the app. Can you recover my workouts?

No. Deleting the app removes its data from your phone, and we hold no copy. This is why keeping a backup matters.

An exercise isn’t in the library.

Open the exercise list and tap Create your own exercise. You can name it, choose a muscle group, and add a photo of the equipment.

How is “total volume” worked out?

Weight × reps for every ticked set, added together. Ten kilograms lifted ten times is 100 kg of volume.

The rest timer, workout clock or tick didn’t behave as expected.

A set ticks by itself once both the weight and reps are filled in, and the rest timer starts then. The workout clock stops after you enter the last set of the workout. If something still looks wrong, email us with what you did.

Health notice

KFM is for general fitness tracking and is not medical advice. Talk to a doctor before starting or changing an exercise program, and stop if you feel pain, dizziness or discomfort.

Privacy Policy

Kingdom Flow Movement (“KFM”) · Last updated 12 November 2026

KFM is a workout tracker. This page explains what the app does with your information. The short version: your information stays on your phone, and we don’t collect any of it.

What stays on your phone

Everything you enter in the app is saved on your device only. That includes your workouts, sets, weights and reps, programs, notes, and any exercises you create.

What we collect

Nothing. KFM has no accounts, no sign-in, and no server that receives your data. It does not include advertising or analytics, and it does not track you or your activity across other apps or websites. We don’t sell or share personal information, because we don’t have any.

Camera and photos

If you choose to add a photo of gym equipment to an exercise you create, KFM asks for permission to use the camera or your photo library. The photo is used only for that exercise and is stored inside the app on your phone. It is never uploaded.

Exercise information

The exercise names, animations and instructions in the app are built in. They are not downloaded and nothing about which ones you view is sent anywhere.

Deleting your information

You can remove your exercises, workouts and programs inside the app. Deleting the app from your phone removes everything it stored. Because we hold no copy, we cannot recover your data if you delete it or lose your phone, so keep a backup if it matters to you.

Links to this page and support

If you open this page or the support page from the app, your browser connects to the website that hosts it, and that site may record ordinary visit information such as your IP address. That is separate from the app itself.

Children

KFM is a general fitness app and is not aimed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Health notice

KFM is for general fitness tracking. It is not medical advice. Talk to a doctor before starting or changing an exercise program, and stop if you feel pain, dizziness or discomfort.

Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that affects your privacy, for example by adding accounts or syncing, we will update this page and the date above before that change reaches you.
